The game: a 2D factory / production-chain game (same family as Factorio or Satisfactory, but played on a board of node cards instead of a map). The player places machine cards (mines, smelters, presses, lathes, assemblers, labs...) and wires their inputs and outputs together into production lines that turn ore into steel, gears, circuit boards and so on. Building a machine costs materials, which the player carries in an inventory bar at the bottom of the screen.

The screen to redesign: the hand-crafting window. The player opens it to make machines and simple parts by hand, using materials from their inventory. Along the top are category tabs (raw materials, metallurgy, forming, machining, assembly, research, storage). The selected category shows its machines and parts as tiles; locked or unaffordable ones are dimmed. Hovering a tile shows its recipe: the input materials with amounts (red when the player doesn't have enough), an arrow, the result and how long it takes. Clicking queues it; crafting takes time.
