You are helping design the inventory menu of a small indie game. Two screenshots are attached:

About the game (FLOWGAME): a calm factory-building game played on a 2D canvas. The player
places machine cards (mines, smelters, rolling mills, presses, assembly lines, labs), links
their outputs to other machines' inputs and grows production chains from raw ore to complex
modules. The long-term goal is to build a "Founding Core" from five modules, which needs many
production lines. The mood we want is warm, bright and tidy, like a well-lit workshop, never
dark and never garish or neon. The current palette is sandstone beige with paper-coloured
panels, a petrol-teal accent and a copper accent.

About this menu (opened with I or C): it is the player's inventory and hand-crafting window
in one.
- Left side: every machine and every product in the game as a grid of slots. Items the player
  carries show their count; items they don't have are faded; items not yet researched have a
  small padlock. Machines and products are listed in two groups.
- Right side: details of the selected item: name, picture, how many the player has, a short
  description, its recipe (input icons with have/need counts, an arrow, the result), the hand
  crafting time, a "Make" button (or, for machine-only products, which machine makes it),
  a "Place" button for machines, and "Used in" icons that jump to the items it goes into.
- Bottom: the crafting queue (items being made with progress bars and a cancel ×), and the
  ten quick-use slots that mirror the hotbar at the bottom of the screen.
